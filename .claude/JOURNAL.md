9. **Task [Extended] - Notebook 7: Wasserstein recalibration closing the prediction gap (Round 6, E12)** (v0.1.0): Diagnosed the ELBO calibration's +0.2/+0.3 period-TFR over-prediction as posterior collapse and fixed it with an information-preserving Wasserstein objective<br>
   **Result**: The user flagged that NB4's ELBO calibration predicted the recent crises wrong - its posterior median rode +0.2 to +0.3 above the observed 2023 TFR - and diagnosed posterior collapse, asking for a Wasserstein loss that preserves mutual information. Reproducing the ELBO in-notebook confirmed it: the per-point Kullback-Leibler term drives the innovation scale τ→0.004 and mutual-information usage to 0.07 (the latent is ignored, the trend flattens into a line that cannot bend to the recent decline). The fix drops the per-point KL for a penalty on the aggregate posterior only - a Wasserstein Auto-Encoder / InfoVAE(α=1) objective. A three-way tournament (ELBO vs RBF-MMD vs exact one-dimensional Wasserstein-2, the sorted-L2 optimal-transport distance) picked exact-W2: it closes the in-sample 2023 gap to 0.018 with MI-usage restored to 0.96 and a 2023 population residual of 0.69%, beating MMD (0.038) and crushing the ELBO (0.198). Out-of-sample period TFR still cannot be point-predicted through the post-2015 regime change, but hierarchical drift-pooling (a cross-region drift spread of 0.0082/yr, the bayesTFR mechanism) plus heavy-tailed Student-t forecast innovations lift held-out TFR coverage 50%→100% at population MAPE 0.65% - honest intervals, not confident misses. Crucially the recalibration does not erase history: all four crisis windows still reproduce with the correct sign (USA recession model −0.239 vs obs −0.257, COVID, Korea IMF, Italy).
