# [Paper digest] The Family 500+ Child Allowance and Female Labour Supply in Poland

**Authors**: Instytut Badan Strukturalnych (IBS)<br>
**Year**: 2018  **Venue**: IBS Working Paper 01/2018<br>
**Original (download)**: [https://ibs.org.pl/app/uploads/2018/03/IBS_Working_Paper_01_2018.pdf](https://ibs.org.pl/app/uploads/2018/03/IBS_Working_Paper_01_2018.pdf)<br>
**Local PDF**: `[paper] Family 500plus female labour supply Poland IBS, 2018.pdf` (in this folder)<br>
**Used in**: side effects (cash reduces female labour supply)

## Summary

Evaluation of the labour-supply response to Poland's universal child benefit.

## Key findings

- The 500+ benefit reduced female labour-force participation via an income effect
- The effect was concentrated among lower-income and less-educated mothers
- It cut child poverty substantially at the same time

## Relevance to this project

Grounds the documented side effect of cash transfers in the interventions doc: cash cuts child poverty but reduces female labour supply - a countervailing interaction.
