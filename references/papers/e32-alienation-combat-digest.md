[paper digest] combating parental alienation - mechanism-transfer donors for a graduated enforcement ladder and a cooperation-reward bond (E32)

Research digest for batch H271-H285. Topic: how to COMBAT parental alienation once it is occurring, using the full toolkit - a graduated/monitored ENFORCEMENT ladder (stick) AND a cooperation-REWARD / bond family (carrot). Every rung's model parameter is calibrated by MECHANISM TRANSFER from an adjacent or unrelated domain that already measured the same behavioural mechanism (deterrence, commitment device, contingency reward, conditional transfer, coercion heterogeneity, removal harm). This is the honest sequel to E31, which modelled criminalisation ONE-SIDED (backfire only). E32 fixes that: a monitored coercion lever has TWO opposing sub-channels - DETERRENCE (fF+) minus BACKFIRE (fScar+/fF-) - and deterrence obeys certainty > severity (Nagin), so only MONITORING, not raw penalty, supplies the deterrent. The contested PA science and the weaponisation risk (Meier 2020, Alsalem 2023) are carried forward unchanged; no donor effect is laundered across the domain gap without stating its limit.

## Channel-transfer logic (how a donor effect size becomes a forcing)

- The model forcings are {fS, fC, fPb, fTau, fRV, fN, fq, fF, fScar}. This batch moves mainly fF (father-access / contact preservation and paternity certainty, feeding the per-cohort intergenerational path integral, 27-45yr compounding lag, selection-discounted per McLanahan 2013), fScar (conflict / relationship-scar, same delayed channel with negative sign), fC (contemporaneous coupling), fS (economic security), and fq (marriageability push)
- A coercion lever is decomposed: net fF = DETERRENCE(+) - BACKFIRE(-), and it simultaneously loads fScar(+) by the weaponisation/conflict it creates. Deterrence scales with CERTAINTY of detection, not severity of penalty (Nagin 2013); monitoring is the only sub-lever that raises certainty
- Transfer validity rule: a donor is admissible only when the underlying behavioural mechanism is the same (immediate certain sanction; refundable stake; milestone reinforcement; conditional transfer; removal LATE). The gap that limits every transfer is population and stakes: co-parenting conflict is not drug use, tax evasion, or classroom effort, so magnitudes are transferred as DIRECTION + bounded size, never as point identity

## ARM A donors - the graduated / monitored ENFORCEMENT ladder (stick)

1. Intensive family-preservation services (IFPS / Homebuilders) - the social-work supervision rung
- Multi-level meta-analysis (2020/2024, ~S0145213420300491): modest overall treatment effect g=0.18; small positive placement effect g=0.31 at child level BUT null at family level; the 1990s randomised trials were "decidedly less promising" than the non-experimental studies; effect appears only under high model fidelity and tight targeting
- Transfer: supervision/support is low-harm and cheap but its measured placement-prevention effect is small-to-null in RCTs. Calibrates the bottom rung as fF small+, fScar ~0, low leakage - a weak, safe, easily-dominated lever
- Limit: IFPS targets placement prevention, not contact restoration; the transfer bounds the SIGN and the smallness, not the exact size

2. Drug courts / therapeutic jurisprudence - the stepped "therapy first, escalate only on failure" rung
- Mitchell, Wilson, Eggers, MacKenzie (2012), Campbell Systematic Reviews, 154 evaluations: adult drug courts cut general recidivism ~12 percentage points (from ~50% to ~38%), drug recidivism ~13pp (to ~37%); mean odds ratio 1.66; effects persist up to 3 years. The active ingredient is graduated sanctions PLUS treatment under judicial monitoring, not treatment alone
- Transfer: a stepped ladder (voluntary co-parenting therapy first, coercion only on documented failure, with a safe-parent screen) inherits the drug-court structure - the escalation THREAT plus treatment, not treatment in isolation. Calibrates fF moderate+, fScar small+ (residual coercion). Distinguishes cleanly from E31's REFUTED forced reunification therapy, whose fault was coercion FIRST and no safe-parent gate
- Limit: drug-court clients self-select and the "safe" comparator is a criminal sanction; the co-parenting analogue must gate on abuse allegations or it re-enters the Meier weaponisation regime

3. HOPE probation + tax-audit certainty - the MONITORED sanction rung (the E31 criminalisation fix)
- Hawken & Kleiman (2009) Hawaii HOPE RCT: swift-certain-fair monitored sanctions cut new arrests ~55%, positive drug tests ~72%, with short certain jail stays. HONEST CAVEAT: the multi-site DFE replications (2016) were largely NULL and the 2009 study was a Level-4 design with incomplete procedural detail - the certainty mechanism is real but the headline magnitude does not replicate
- Slemrod, Blumenthal & Christian (2001), J Public Economics 79:455-483, Minnesota audit-letter field experiment: telling taxpayers their return would be "closely examined" raised reported payments for low/middle-income filers with evasion opportunity; effect concentrated exactly where detection certainty had bite; high-income self-employed responded perversely (reported LESS). Certainty deters the deterrable and can backfire where the target can out-manoeuvre it
- Transfer: monitoring supplies the CERTAINTY that raw criminalisation cannot, so the deterrence sub-term fF+ becomes non-zero under monitoring and ~zero without it. Net of this rung = DETERRENCE(fF+, certainty-scaled) - BACKFIRE(fScar+, fF- from weaponisation). The tax perverse-response and HOPE null-replication together bound the net near zero and context-dependent, NOT reliably positive
- Limit: probation/tax subjects have a clean objective signal (drug test, third-party income report); "alienation occurred" has no such clean signal, which is the H283 design problem

4. Minneapolis / SARP mandatory arrest - the coercion sign-heterogeneity caution
- Sherman & Berk (1984) Minneapolis: arrest deterred misdemeanour domestic assault vs mediation/separation. The five NIJ-funded Spouse Assault Replication Program studies (Omaha, Charlotte, Milwaukee, Metro-Dade, Colorado Springs, 1985-1990) then split: arrest DETERRED employed suspects but ESCALATED reoffending among unemployed/unmarried suspects. Sherman (1992) concluded mandatory pro-arrest policy was inadvisable because its sign is population-dependent
- Transfer: the monitored-sanction lever's SIGN flips by subpopulation - net fF+ for resource-secure cooperative-capable cases, net fF-/fScar+ (escalation) for low-resource high-conflict cases. Forbids a blanket coercion setting; calibrates a heterogeneity term, not a mean
- Limit: employment is a proxy for stakes-in-conformity; the co-parenting analogue is disputed-abuse vs benign-withholding, but the lesson (coercion escalates where the target has little to lose) transfers directly

5. Doyle foster care + ECHR Strand Lobben - the removal-until-reconciliation rung (expected harmful)
- Doyle (2007, AER 97(5):1583-1610; 2008, JPE 116(4)): using rotational investigator assignment as an instrument, children at the MARGIN of placement have WORSE long-run outcomes when removed - higher juvenile delinquency and adult crime, more teen motherhood, lower earnings/employment; older marginal children fare better left at home
- ECHR Grand Chamber, Strand Lobben and Others v. Norway (2019), plus a string of subsequent Article 8 violations against Norway's Barnevernet: removal/adoption decisions repeatedly found to breach the right to family life
- Transfer: removal-until-reconciliation SEVERS BOTH parental bonds and adds placement trauma - the opposite of contact preservation. Calibrates fF strongly NEGATIVE (severs), fScar strongly POSITIVE, negative intergenerational dividend, high leakage. Expected REFUTED/harmful
- Limit: Doyle's margin is maltreatment investigation, not custody enforcement; the transfer is the direction and the marginal-case harm, which is if anything conservative here because alienation cases are lower-risk than maltreatment cases

## ARM B donors - the cooperation-REWARD / bond family (carrot)

6. CARES deposit contract - the refund + clawback bond (the user's contrarian seed)
- Gine, Karlan & Zinman (2010), AEJ: Applied Economics 2(4):213-235, "Put Your Money Where Your Butt Is": smokers deposited their own money for 6 months, refunded on passing a nicotine urine test, forfeited to charity on failure. Take-up 11%; those offered were ~3 percentage points more likely to pass at 6 months (a large ~30-40% relative lift on a low base); effect persisted in a surprise 12-month test
- Transfer: refund-both-sides-court-fees-if-stable / claw-back-if-instability is a deposit-commitment device with loss-aversion on the clawback. Because BOTH parents post/earn the same stake, the instrument is symmetric and un-weaponisable - the key defection-robustness property that raw criminalisation lacks. Calibrates fF+, fScar- (de-escalates), fC small+; LOW leakage
- Limit: low take-up (11%) means it self-selects the motivated; it cannot compel the committed alienator, so it is a preventive/maintenance lever not a rescue lever

7. Contingency management in addiction - milestone micro-rewards
- Meta-analyses (e.g. PMC8034391, 2021): contingency management has the LARGEST effect size of any psychosocial treatment for reducing drug use during treatment (Cohen's g ~0.54), robust across substances and demographics, with immediate tangible reinforcement on each objective negative test. Higgins (cocaine vouchers) and Petry (prize CM) established the paradigm
- HONEST CAVEAT: the effect attenuates after reinforcement stops - CM works strongly WHILE the reward flows and partially fades afterward, the same durability problem as voluntary marriageability (fq fade)
- Transfer: small immediate rewards for each observed co-parenting milestone (on-time exchanges, completed handovers) inherit CM's strong contemporaneous effect and its post-reward fade. Calibrates fF moderate+ (contemporaneous), fScar-; durability discounted
- Limit: CM reinforces an objectively measurable behaviour; co-parenting cooperation is only partly objectively measurable, capping the transferable size

8. Progresa / Oportunidades + Bolsa Familia - conditional cash / tax credit for sustained co-parenting
- Schultz (2004), J Development Economics, Mexico Progresa RCT: conditioning transfers on school attendance raised enrollment ~3.4 percentage points on average, up to ~9pp at the junior-secondary transition (largest for girls), ~0.66 added years of schooling. Bolsa Escola/Familia (Brazil) confirmed enrollment/retention gains at national scale
- Transfer: a transfer conditioned on sustained cooperation moves the conditioned behaviour reliably. BUT on this campaign's own cost axis, cash is the weakest fertility lever (E15/E20 - cash is a mirage), so the conditional-cash rung buys cooperation compliance without a proportional fertility dividend. Calibrates fS+, fF small+; PARTIAL
- Limit: CCT conditionality changes attendance-type behaviours; transferring to a relational outcome (genuine low-conflict co-parenting vs merely logged compliance) is the gaming risk that caps validity

9. Fryer, Levitt, List & Sadoff (2012) - loss-frame vs gain-frame
- NBER WP 18237 / AEJ:Policy, Chicago Heights teacher-incentive field experiment: paying the bonus IN ADVANCE and clawing it back on failure (loss framing) raised maths scores ~0.20-0.40 SD; the identical GAIN-framed bonus produced smaller, statistically insignificant effects
- Transfer: the clawback (lose-it) framing of the co-parenting bond should out-perform an equal-cost earn-it (gain) reward at zero extra fiscal cost - a pure design free-lunch. Calibrates a framing multiplier on the ARM-B bond, not a new channel
- Limit: loss framing on custody money could itself raise conflict if it feels punitive; the framing gain is real but must be capped so the clawback does not become a weaponisable penalty (folds back into H283)

## Cross-cutting: certainty > severity, selection discount, weaponisation (carried from E31)

- Nagin (2013) and the deterrence literature: the certainty of detection deters; raising penalty severity does not. This is why raw criminalisation (severity) has ~zero deterrence sub-term while monitoring (certainty) has a real one - the axis on which H273 and H274 diverge
- McLanahan, Tach & Schneider (2013): father-absence effects are partly causal, partly selection - so every fF+ dividend into the intergenerational path integral is SELECTION-DISCOUNTED (~0.5-0.7), not taken at face value
- Meier et al. (2020) and Alsalem (2023): the same enforcement machinery that preserves benign father contact hands an abuser a custody weapon; fathers' alienation cross-claims roughly double a protective mother's risk of losing custody. Every coercion lever must stack this DV-overlap due-process cost. The symmetric two-sided bond (ARM B) is the one design that removes the weaponisation asymmetry, because both parents hold the same stake
