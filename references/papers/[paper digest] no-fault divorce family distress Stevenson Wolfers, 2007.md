## Summary

Analysis of long-run US marriage and divorce trends and the effect of divorce-law reform.

## Key findings

- Unilateral (no-fault) divorce raised divorce rates sharply for ~2 years, then reverted; no discernible effect a decade on
- No-fault divorce is associated with lower female suicide (8-16%) and ~30% less domestic violence
- Divorce law shifts bargaining power within marriage rather than durably changing dissolution

## Relevance to this project

Grounds the REFUTED verdict on H80: tightening divorce law to force stability does not durably lower divorce and repeal would harm women - a backfiring coupling lever.
