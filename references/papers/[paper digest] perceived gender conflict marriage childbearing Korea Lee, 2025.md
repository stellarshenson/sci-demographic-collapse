## Summary

Survey study linking perceived gender conflict to young Koreans' marriage and childbearing attitudes.

## Key findings

- Perceived gender conflict is negatively associated with young Koreans' marriage and childbearing attitudes
- Context: Korea's 'gender war', the 4B movement (rejecting marriage/childbirth/dating/sex), and a widening young-adult gender divide
- Men's top answer to what women should 'fix' to be marriageable was 'feminism' - a values chasm suppressing matches
