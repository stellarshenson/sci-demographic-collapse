# [Paper digest] Till Porn Do Us Part? A Longitudinal Examination of Pornography Use and Divorce

**Original (source)**: [https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/00224499.2017.1317709](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/00224499.2017.1317709) (DOI 10.1080/00224499.2017.1317709)<br>

**Grounds**: E17-H134 (porn -> divorce, the counter-channel to the partnership story)

## Key mechanism

Beginning pornography use during a marriage predicts subsequent divorce, using within-person change across survey waves rather than cross-sectional correlation. The three-wave panel lets the authors ask whether starting (or stopping) porn use precedes dissolution, tightening the causal read relative to one-shot studies.

## Main findings

- Married Americans who began pornography use between waves roughly doubled their probability of divorce (odds ratio ~2.19; N = 2,120; GSS panels 2006-2014, three waves)
- Discontinuing pornography use between waves was associated with lower divorce probability - but only for women
- The begin-porn -> divorce link was strongest among younger, less religious respondents and those reporting greater initial marital happiness

## Key takeaways

- A genuine porn -> divorce channel exists at the couple level and is not trivially small (risk roughly doubles)
- Sits in tension with Twenge 2017: the aggregate "sex recession" is partnership decline, yet within marriages porn onset still raises dissolution risk - two distinct mechanisms, not one
- Together they bound H134: porn is a marital-stability risk factor, but not the driver of population-level declining sexual frequency
