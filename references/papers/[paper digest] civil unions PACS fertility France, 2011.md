## Summary

Studies of France's PACS civil union (introduced 1999) and its links to union formation and fertility.

## Key findings

- Before 1999 marriage and fertility moved together; after the PACS that link weakened
- But legal unions defined as marriage plus PACS continue to move with fertility
- France carries 2.5M cohabiting couples and over 40% non-marital births - broad recognition of non-marital unions

## Relevance to this project

Grounds H78: legally recognizing cohabitation (rather than privileging marriage) keeps the union-fertility link intact. PDF paywalled (Springer) - link to the article.
