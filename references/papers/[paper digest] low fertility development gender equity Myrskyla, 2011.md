## Summary

The development J-curve: fertility falls then rebounds at high development, but only where gender equity is high.

## Key findings

- Very-high-development, low-gender-equity societies (Japan, Korea, Hong Kong) are outliers with ultra-low fertility
- Without a change in the gender regime, fertility does not rebound even as development rises
- The rebound appears where female employment is the norm (France, Scandinavia, US)
