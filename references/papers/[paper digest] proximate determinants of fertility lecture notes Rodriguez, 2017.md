# Proximate determinants of fertility (Bongaarts model) - lecture notes

**German Rodriguez, 2017** (Princeton POP502). Distills Bongaarts 1978/1982.

Download: https://grodri.github.io/demography/ProximateDeterminants.pdf

## What it establishes
- The canonical MULTIPLICATIVE combination law for fertility determinants:
  TFR = Cm * Cc * Ca * Ci * TF, each index in (0,1], TF ~ 15.3 the maximum potential.
  This is the direct lineage of our TFR = C*(1-rho)*Pbar*fec(tau)*(1-kBF dtau): a product of
  bounded channel factors.
- Each index is a ratio of fertility rates; independent determinants multiply. Taking logs makes the
  determinants ADDITIVE: log TFR = log TF + sum_c log C_c - the log-additive orthogonal-channel
  baseline of our law.
- Bongaarts' decomposition of a CHANGE in TFR across several indices carries an explicit INTERACTION
  factor I (the proportional change does not split cleanly because the model is multiplicative) -
  the demographic precedent for treating cross-channel interaction as the residual off log-additivity.

## Grounds
The log-additive main-effect baseline (Bongaarts multiplicativity) and the precedent that combining
indices produces an interaction term, not clean additivity, on the raw scale.
