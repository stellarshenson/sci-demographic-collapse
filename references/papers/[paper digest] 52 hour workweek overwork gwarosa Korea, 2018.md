## Summary

Korea's long-hours work culture and its effect on family formation.

## Key findings

- Korea capped the week at 52 hours but still works ~2,069 hours/year, second in the OECD
- Long hours crowd out marriage and childrearing; 'a baby means the end of your career'
- Women working 35-45 hours/week have higher pregnancy odds; fathers' long hours prevent co-parenting
