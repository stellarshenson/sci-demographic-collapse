## Summary

Review of the evidence on how student loan debt shapes family formation.

## Key findings

- Student debt delays marriage, homeownership and (for some) childbearing
- Women with debt are more likely to delay marriage or remain single/cohabiting
- Financial distress from debt reduces marriage and childbearing among young adults

## Relevance to this project

Grounds H72: student-debt / education-cost relief pulls family formation earlier by removing a documented delay driver.
