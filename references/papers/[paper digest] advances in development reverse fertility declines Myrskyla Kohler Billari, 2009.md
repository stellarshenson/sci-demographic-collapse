# Advances in Development Reverse Fertility Declines - Myrskyla, Kohler, Billari (2009)

**Nature 460, 741-743.** OA UN-EGM slide deck in this folder; follow-up on the gender-equity condition
is Myrskyla-Kohler-Billari (2011, MPIDR WP-2011-017) and Myrskyla et al. (2013, *Demography* 51(1),
173-200 "A Reversal in the Relationship of Human Development With Fertility?").

## Key mechanism - the J-shaped development-fertility curve

Across countries, higher development lowers TFR up to a point, then REVERSES: above a development
threshold, further development RAISES fertility. This is the cross-national empirical basis for a
recuperation term and, crucially, for GATING it.

- **Reversal threshold: HDI ~= 0.86** (maximum-likelihood turning point 0.8598). Below 0.86, higher
  HDI -> lower TFR; above 0.86, higher HDI -> higher TFR - the J-shape / upturn.
- **Magnitude**: at high development an extra ~0.25 HDI points maps to roughly **+1 child per woman** -
  a large quantum swing, same order as the observed 2000-2019 recoveries.
- **Gender equality is a NECESSARY condition** (2011 follow-up): the high-development upturn appears
  ONLY where gender parity is high. High-HDI / LOW-gender-equity societies (Korea, Japan, southern
  Europe) do NOT reverse - they keep declining. The reversal is driven mainly by rising fertility at
  OLDER reproductive ages (the recuperation of postponed births).

## Key takeaways for the model

- **The gate that spares Korea.** A recuperation term gated on gender equity fires for Germany
  (high HDI, high gender equity, post-2007 Elterngeld/childcare) but stays SHUT for Korea (high HDI,
  low gender equity) - preserving Korea's monotone-collapse backtest gate BY CONSTRUCTION, not by a
  hand-tuned per-region switch. This is the mechanistically-grounded discriminator between the
  recovering regions and the collapsing one.
- The +1-child-per-0.25-HDI slope and the ~0.86 threshold anchor the strength and the trigger of a
  development/gender-equity-gated quantum rebound.
- Consistent with Goldstein-Sobotka-Jasilioniene (2009, already in library): the rebound is a QUANTUM
  swing at older ages while MAC keeps rising - the E46 signature.
