[paper digest] religion and fertility - spectrums, denominations and the channels they actually run through (E33)

Research digest for batch H298-H313. Topic: RELIGION and fertility fanned out across its spectrums (affiliation vs intensity, denomination gradient, bounded high-fertility subcultures, pronatalist theology, state establishment, the Israel outlier, secularisation at the edge) and its MECHANISMS (the model channels each religion effect runs through). The honest throughline, stated up front so it is not laundered: at population scale religion is almost never a primitive lever. It is a BUNDLE that loads channels the model already owns - the pronatal norm N, marriage/coupling C, parity Pbar, tempo tau, childlessness rho, community/meaning S - plus two things the model treats specially: a bounded high-fertility group is SUBPOPULATION COMPOUNDING (its own Leslie sub-population with lambda>1), not a forcing; and religious transmission feeds the E30 intergenerational path integral through RETENTION. It is intensity (practice) and retention, not nominal affiliation, that carry the signal.

## Channel-mapping logic (how a religion effect becomes a forcing, or is flagged as compounding)

- Model forcings {fS, fC, fPb, fTau, fRV, fN, fq, fF, fScar}. Religion's measured fertility gap decomposes across these; the analytic task is attribution, not a new primitive. Where the gap survives controls for norm, marriage, tempo and gender ideology, a residual "religion per se" term is small
- A BOUNDED SUBCULTURE (Amish, Hutterite, Haredi) is NOT a channel forcing. It is a distinct Leslie sub-population growing at lambda>1; the compounding lever is RETENTION (1 - apostasy), not any single behavioural push. The model must represent these as a compounding sub-population with a leakage rate, not as fN/fPb dials applied to the whole population
- RETENTION ties to the E30 path integral: parents transmit religiosity to the next cohort, and only the retained fraction inherits the high-fertility behaviour. Secularisation at the edge (apostasy) is the delta that bleeds the subculture and bounds its long-run share
- Attribution honesty: Iran fell 6.6 -> 1.35 while remaining an Islamic theocracy (Abbasi-Shavazi; bne IntelliNews 2025); Utah LDS fell 3.31 -> ~1.8 (Kem Gardner Institute). Religion did not hold fertility when the surrounding norm and security moved. So the transferable religion effect is the NORM and COMMUNITY it supplies, gated by existential insecurity (Norris-Inglehart), not doctrine per se

## SPECTRUM 1 - affiliation vs intensity (the load-bearing distinction)

- Frejka & Westoff (2008), European Journal of Population 24:5-31: "Religion, Religiousness and Fertility in the US and in Europe." In every European region and the US, and within every denomination, the MORE DEVOUT have more children. Denomination alone (Catholic/Protestant vs none) raises fertility, but the within-denomination devoutness gradient is the sharper signal. Caveat they themselves report: in multivariate models for Europe as a WHOLE, church attendance was not significant while "importance of religion in daily life" was; attendance stayed significant in Southern Europe. So the intensity measure that matters is salience, not raw attendance count
- US GSS, 1982-2019, ~70,000+ women (Stone / IFS "America's Growing Religious-Secular Fertility Divide"): weekly-attending women never fell much below ~2.0 and were ~2.4 as recently as 2008; religiously affiliated but less-than-weekly attenders sit clearly lower; unaffiliated lowest. Post-2016 the weekly and nominal groups DIVERGE - fertility fell among the nominally religious while weekly attenders held. This is the cleanest evidence that PRACTICE, not identity, carries the effect
- Zhang (2008) and Hout-Greeley-Wilde tradition: monthly+ attendance and biblical literalism associate with slower-than-average fertility decline (GSS 1972-2016)

## SPECTRUM 2 - denomination gradient (global, Pew/Hackett)

- Pew Research Center / Conrad Hackett, "The Future of World Religions" (2015) and "The Changing Global Religious Landscape" (2017), global TFR 2010-2015: Muslim 2.9-3.1 (highest), Christian 2.6-2.7, Hindu 2.3, Jewish 2.3 (aggregate, masks Haredi-secular bimodality), folk/other ~1.6-1.8, religiously UNAFFILIATED 1.6 (well below the world average 2.4). Ordering: high-boundary/high-practice groups top, unaffiliated bottom
- Within-country the between-religion gap shrinks sharply once development and region are controlled (Pew "Main Factors Driving Population Growth"): most of the global Muslim-Christian-unaffiliated ordering is a proxy for where the group lives and how poor/insecure it is, not theology. Iran (Muslim, TFR 1.35) and Utah (LDS, ~1.8) both sit below secular France
- US denomination ordering (Frejka-Westoff; Pew Religious Landscape): historically LDS/Mormon and conservative/evangelical Protestant highest among the mainstream, mainline Protestant and Catholic middling and converging down, Jewish bimodal, unaffiliated lowest - but the conservative-Protestant advantage has been ERODING (Hackett; DeRose 2021), converging toward the mainstream

## SPECTRUM 3 - bounded high-fertility subcultures (SUBPOPULATION COMPOUNDING, not a lever)

- Hutterites: Eaton & Mayer (1954) documented completed family size ~10.9 (women 45-49) and TFR 9.99 for 1946-1950 - the highest reliably recorded NATURAL fertility, adopted by Coale as the index-of-marital-fertility standard. Population grew 443 -> 8,542 (1880-1950), ~4.12%/yr, then the fastest natural growth on record. Later Dariusleut colonies show decline once birth control entered (Nonaka; Peter) - even the archetype is not immune
- Amish (Old Order): Greksa & Korbin (2002), Annals of Human Biology 29:192-201, Geauga OH - completed marital fertility ~7.7, population doubling ~ every 20 years. RETENTION is the second engine: baptism/retention now ~85-90% (up from <60% in the 1960s); exit fell from ~21-30% for 1930s birth cohorts to ~5% for 1960s cohorts (Anabaptist World; Amish Studies, Etown). Horse-and-buggy Amish grew ~97% since 2000
- Haredi (Israel): TFR 6.6, stable ~a decade (Jerusalem Institute for Policy Research; Israel CBS via Times of Israel). Israel Democracy Institute "Statistical Report on Ultra-Orthodox Society 2024" tracks the compounding share
- The mechanism is compounding x retention x endogamy, NOT a policy a state can copy. Flag all three in the model as Leslie sub-populations with lambda>1 and an apostasy leakage delta, not as channel forcings on the general population

## SPECTRUM 4 - pronatalist theology gradient

- Explicit pronatalism: Quiverfull (Psalm 127 "arrows in a quiver"), evangelical, rejects ALL contraception including NFP, bundles biblical patriarchy + homeschooling + "multigenerational faithfulness" (McGowin, Quivering Families; Joyce, Quiverfull). LDS "multiply and replenish"; Haredi doctrinal pronatalism. These sit at the theology-maximal end
- The theology-vs-practice gap: doctrine sets a ceiling, but realised fertility tracks PRACTICE and community, not stated theology. Catholic doctrine bans contraception yet Catholic TFR converged to the secular mean across Europe and Latin America; the doctrine did not bind. So pronatalist theology is real but its effect is mediated entirely through the norm N and parity Pbar it actually induces in practice - it is not a separate channel
- Kaufmann (2010), Shall the Religious Inherit the Earth?: "the stricter and more observant the group, the more fertile" - the gradient is monotonic in strictness/observance, consistent with theology acting through enforced norm intensity rather than propositional content

## SPECTRUM 5 - state / establishment religion

- Norris & Inglehart (2004), Sacred and Secular: existential-security thesis. Established churches and state religious support do NOT sustain religiosity or fertility where security is high - Europe's established churches (Church of England, Nordic Lutheran state churches) coexist with the lowest religiosity and fertility. Establishment is close to inert as a fertility lever; organic, high-cost, high-boundary religiosity is what carries fertility, and that is precisely what an established state church is not
- Religious schooling can shift transmission/retention at the margin (feeds the E30 path integral) but the establishment cases show it does not manufacture the norm where security has dissolved it. Bounds a state-establishment lever near zero on fertility, small-positive on retention only

## SPECTRUM 6 - the Israel outlier

- Taub Center / Alex Weinreb: Israel TFR ~3.0, highest in the OECD by far (Times of Israel; Taub "Israel's Exceptional Fertility"). The outlier is NOT the Haredi tail - it is that the ~75% of Jewish Israelis who are NOT religious/ultra-Orthodox have TFR ABOVE replacement (~2.0-2.2 secular, higher for traditional/"masorti")
- Weinreb's mechanism: NATIONALISM supplies a secular alternative source of traditionalist, family-oriented commitment and a "shared identity across time" - a pronatal NORM decoupled from personal piety, backed by near-universal marriage, strong kin support, and state-subsidised IVF (most fertility treatments per capita in the world). This is the cleanest case that the transferable ingredient is the NORM + COMMUNITY + SECURITY bundle (fN down, fS up, fC up), not religion per se - a secular society reproduced the religious fertility norm through nationalism

## SPECTRUM 7 - secularisation / the edge (retention vs apostasy)

- Kaufmann (2010): high-fertility sects are "endogenous growth" groups - they grow their own and retain them; some leak but "not nearly enough to halt expansion." The compounding lever is retention, and the delta is apostasy at the boundary
- Honest counter (Jason Collins; EA Forum retrospective on Kaufmann): the evidence for high retention is WEAKER and more context-dependent than Kaufmann assumed; the fertility gap is robust but retention is the fragile assumption. Secularising individuals at the edge revert to mainstream fertility within a generation, capping the projected takeover. Iran (6.6 -> 1.35) and Utah LDS (3.31 -> 1.8) are the macro proof that the norm secularises faster than doctrine holds
- Developed-world religiosity is declining (Norris-Inglehart; Pew 2025 religious-switching), which lowers the affiliated share the fertility advantage applies to - a shrinking base offsets a stable per-capita gap

## MECHANISM MAP - which channel each religion effect runs through

- Pronatal NORM (fN down): the strongest and most transferable single channel; the bistable N built in E25 is exactly this. But the attribution finding (Israel-nationalism, Iran-collapse) says it is the NORM that transfers, not religion - religion is one carrier of the norm, not the only one
- MARRIAGE / coupling (fC up) + lower divorce (fScar down): religious populations marry earlier, more universally, and divorce less (DeRose 2021; couple-stability literature). A large part of the religious fertility gap is compositional through marriage, a channel the model already owns
- CONTRACEPTION / PARITY (fPb up): natural-fertility sects (Amish, Hutterite, Quiverfull) raise Pbar by non-stopping; mainstream religious couples show smaller parity effects
- EARLIER TEMPO (fTau down): earlier marriage and first birth in observant populations; a tempo shift, partly a quantum shift in sects
- LOWER CHILDLESSNESS (fRV down): near-universal reproduction in high-fertility sects (rho -> ~0); the strongest quantum contribution in the compounding groups
- COMMUNITY / SOCIAL CAPITAL / MEANING (fS up): Shaver et al. (2020), Phil Trans R Soc B, ALSPAC 10-yr English mothers - church attendance raises social support and CO-RELIGIONIST aid, and co-religionist aid positively predicts fertility, while secular network support decays over time. The congregation is an alloparenting / uncertainty-buffering institution - this is the fS "meaning-and-community" buffer, empirically identified
- INTERGENERATIONAL TRANSMISSION / RETENTION: parents transmit religiosity -> retained children inherit the high-fertility behaviour; the E30 path integral with retention as the compounding coefficient. Retention is the real lever for the bounded subcultures and the fragile assumption per the Kaufmann critique
- GENDER-ROLE INTERACTION: DeRose et al. (2021), Population and Development Review 47:41-55, "Gender Equity, Religion, and Fertility": the fertility advantage of TRADITIONAL gender ideology has shrunk over time, yet the RELIGIOUS fertility advantage barely moved - religious fertility is resilient even as its traditional-gender-role component erodes. Goldscheider/Myrskyla gender-revolution logic: egalitarian religious couples who share the second shift can sustain fertility. So religious fertility today is largely DESPITE, not because of, gender traditionalism - consistent with the campaign's prior finding that traditional roles backfire and female-income flips positive under gender-equity-in-the-home (Doepke-Kindermann)

## INTERACTION NOTES (for the notebook build)

- religion x N: much of the religion effect IS the norm channel. Test religion with N held fixed - the residual "religion per se" term should be small; Israel-nationalism and Iran-collapse are the identification that the norm is separable from and dominates doctrine
- religion x gender-equity: DESPITE not because. Traditional-role religious fertility is fading (DeRose 2021); the durable religious advantage survives via norm + community + marriage, and egalitarian religious couples do best. Do NOT model religion as a traditional-gender-role forcing - that channel already tested as backfiring
- bounded-subculture x retention: this is the real compounding lever and it is a SUBPOPULATION effect, not a forcing. Model Amish/Hutterite/Haredi as Leslie sub-populations lambda>1 with apostasy leakage delta; the Kaufmann critique says delta is larger and more fragile than assumed - stress-test the takeover projection against a doubled apostasy rate
- religion x intergenerational path integral: retention is the coefficient on the E30 transmission term; secularisation at the edge is the leak. A high per-capita fertility gap with a rising apostasy delta yields a bounded, not explosive, long-run share
