# [Paper digest] Who Benefits from Gender-Neutral Tenure Clock Stopping Policies?

**Authors**: Heather Antecol, Kelly Bedard, Jenna Stearns<br>
**Year**: 2018  **Venue**: IZA / American Economic Review 2018<br>
**Original (download)**: [https://conference.iza.org/conference_files/Gender_2018/stearns_j23554.pdf](https://conference.iza.org/conference_files/Gender_2018/stearns_j23554.pdf)<br>

## Summary

Effect of gender-neutral tenure-clock-stopping policies in US economics departments.

## Key findings

- Gender-neutral clock stopping raised men's tenure rates and lowered women's
- It widened the gender promotion gap, because men used the extra time to publish while women did caregiving
- A gender-neutral design can deepen the penalty it intends to remove
