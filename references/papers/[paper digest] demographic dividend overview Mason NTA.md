# Overview of the Demographic Dividend (Andrew Mason, NTA Working Group, 2013; after Lee & Mason 2006)

**0.2-0.9% per year** extra economic growth from the FIRST demographic dividend; **two** distinct dividends (a transitory age-structure one and a durable asset one); biggest gains in East and SE Asia, small gains where fertility fell slowly.

## Key mechanism

The first demographic dividend is a pure age-structure effect. As fertility falls, the working-age share rises faster than the dependent (young + old) population for a few decades, so the support ratio - effective producers over effective consumers - rises and per-capita output gets a temporary boost, freeing resources for investment and family welfare (Lee & Mason 2006). It is explicitly TRANSITORY: the same transition that opens the window later closes it, and as the population ages (old-age dependency rises) the support ratio falls and the first dividend turns into a drag. The second demographic dividend is the behavioural response to that aging: a population concentrated at older working ages, facing a long retirement, has a strong incentive to accumulate assets, permanently raising the capital stock unless families or the state crowd it out.

## Main findings

- First-dividend boost to growth ranges 0.2-0.9% per annum across countries; largest in East / SE Asia, smallest where the fertility decline was slow
- The dividend is a window, not a permanent gift - it is transitory and reverses as the age structure matures and old-age dependency climbs
- The support ratio (effective workers / effective consumers) is the operative quantity, built from age profiles of labour income and consumption

## Key takeaways for the campaign

- The age-structure effect on household resources is SIGNED and SYMMETRIC in principle: a younger / maturing pyramid is a tailwind, an aging pyramid is a headwind. The model's `dep_pen = dep_fb * max(dep - dep0, 0)` keeps only the headwind (the aging drag) and clips the tailwind (DEF-2)
- BUT the dividend is a slow, second-order, multi-decade phenomenon - the tailwind branch only bites once an intervention actually reverses a pyramid, which no region did over the 2000-2023 backtest window, so restoring the symmetry is inert on the hindcast (grounds DEF-2's REFUTED-AS-DEFECT / forward-only verdict)
