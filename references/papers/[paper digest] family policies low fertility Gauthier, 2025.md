## Summary

A reflective review of the evidence on family policy and fertility in low-fertility countries.

## Key findings

- Childcare and in-kind support outperform cash on completed fertility
- Cash effects are mixed and often temporary/tempo
- Policy taxonomy by mechanism used to classify levers by the margin they act on
