# [Paper digest] Cash Transfers and Fertility: Evidence from Poland's Family 500+ Policy

**Authors**: et al.<br>
**Year**: 2024  **Venue**: Demographic Research 51(28)<br>
**Original (download)**: [https://www.demographic-research.org/volumes/vol51/28/51-28.pdf](https://www.demographic-research.org/volumes/vol51/28/51-28.pdf)<br>
**Local PDF**: `[paper] Family 500plus cash transfer fertility Poland, 2024.pdf` (in this folder)<br>
**Used in**: the Poland lesson; E15-H51 (cash weak)

## Summary

Impact analysis of Poland's large universal child-benefit (Family 500+, later 800+) on birth dynamics.

## Key findings

- The program modestly raised fertility in the short term, varying by age and income
- The total fertility rate nonetheless fell overall (1.32 in 2015 -> ~1.1 by 2025)
- Financial incentives alone were not enough to reverse the decline

## Relevance to this project

The core of the Poland lesson in `docs/interventions.md`: free nurseries plus a 93-billion-euro cash program did not durably lift fertility, because they miss the career-first arms race.
