## Summary

Cross-national analysis of the relationship between union separation, repartnering and cumulated fertility.

## Key findings

- A negative separation-fertility relationship, partly offset by repartnering in some contexts
- Time in a union during the reproductive lifespan is a key determinant of cumulated fertility
- Offsetting is weaker after age ~30

## Relevance to this project

Corroborates H59 across countries: union stability / time-in-union raises completed fertility.
