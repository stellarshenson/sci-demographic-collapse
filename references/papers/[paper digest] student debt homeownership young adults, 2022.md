# [Paper digest] Student Loan Debt and the Transition to Adulthood (debt, homeownership and family formation)

**Authors**: (Fed / IZA literature; Mezza et al., Nau-Dwyer-Hodson)<br>
**Year**: 2022  **Venue**: IZA Discussion Paper 15039 (and related Fed work)<br>
**Original (download)**: [https://docs.iza.org/dp15039.pdf](https://docs.iza.org/dp15039.pdf)<br>

## Summary

Evidence on how student-loan debt delays the transitions of early adulthood - homeownership, marriage and childbearing.

## Key findings

- Each +\$1,000 of student debt lowers mid-20s homeownership by ~1.8 pp (about a 4-month delay)
- Student debt is linked to ~400,000 fewer young homeowners (~25% of the homeownership decline)
- High student-loan balances delay female fertility specifically (Nau-Dwyer-Hodson, NLSY97)
- The burden is an institutional-tuition arms race, not a household choice

## Relevance to this project

Grounds H115: capping/forgiving tuition removes an institutional resource-sink and is a structural (defection-robust) lever that pulls family formation earlier - a Western-specific driver the earlier Korea-heavy analysis under-weighted.
