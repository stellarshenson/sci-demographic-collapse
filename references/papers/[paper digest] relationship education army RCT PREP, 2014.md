## Summary

RCT of the PREP couple relationship-education program among 662 US Army married couples, two-year follow-up.

## Key findings

- No overall enduring effect on relationship quality
- At the higher-risk site, intervention couples were less likely to divorce (8.1% vs 14.9%) at two years
- The divorce-prevention effect was strongest for ethnic-minority couples

## Relevance to this project

Grounds the PARTIAL verdict on H83: relationship-skills education prevents divorce for high-risk couples but does nothing on average - a targeted, not universal, coupling lever. Render-blocked - link to PMC.
