# [Paper digest] Union Instability as an Engine of Fertility? A Microsimulation Model for France

**Authors**: Elizabeth Thomson, Maria Winkler-Dworak, Laurent Toulemon, et al.<br>
**Year**: 2012  **Venue**: Demography 49(1)<br>
**Original (download)**: [https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC3271207?pdf=render](https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC3271207?pdf=render)<br>
**Local PDF**: `[paper] union instability engine of fertility Toulemon, 2012.pdf` (in this folder)<br>
**Used in**: E15-H59 (longer / stable unions - the time-in-union twist)

## Summary

Microsimulation of how union dissolution and repartnering shape completed cohort fertility in France.

## Key findings

- Dissolution during childbearing years lowers completed fertility on average
- But repartnering raises parity progression, partly offsetting the loss - 'instability as an engine'
- Time spent in a union during the reproductive span is the key determinant

## Relevance to this project

Grounds H59 and its honest twist: the lever is time-in-union, not marriage per se, because repartnering generates new-union births.
