## Key mechanism

An accessible exit option (unilateral / no-fault divorce) shifts bargaining power inside a marriage. The credible threat to leave lets a spouse escape or renegotiate an abusive or miserable union, so distress falls even among couples who never divorce. Divorce and suicide are partial substitutes: the option value of leaving alive replaces the extreme exit.

## Main findings

- Female suicide fell ~20% in states that adopted unilateral divorce; no discernible effect on male suicide
- Domestic violence fell ~4.3 percentage points, about one-third, over 1976-85 in reform states, for both men and women
- Females murdered by intimate partners declined, though the timing evidence is weaker; no effect on males murdered
- Quasi-experimental identification from the staggered timing of state divorce-law reform on state panel data

## Key takeaways

- Widening the outside option is protective: the valve reduces distress in the stock of marriages, not just dissolves bad ones
- Symmetric to the library's 2007 Stevenson-Wolfers digest, which shows the divorce-rate spike is transient - together they say restricting exit (covenant marriage) backfires
- The lever is exit access as a bargaining chip, not divorce itself
