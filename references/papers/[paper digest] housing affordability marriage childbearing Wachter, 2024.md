## Summary

Analysis of how housing cost and affordability shape co-residence, marriage and childbearing timing.

## Key findings

- Higher-cost, less-affordable markets show more co-residence and delayed marriage/childbearing
- Young adults postpone milestones until reaching economic/housing security
- Family-size (3BR+) supply matters more than a larger quantity of small units

## Relevance to this project

Grounds H73 (family-size housing raises births more than an equal spend on small units) and the housing lever generally.
