# [Paper digest] Temporal Trends in Sperm Count: A Systematic Review and Meta-Regression Analysis

**Authors**: Hagai Levine, Shanna Swan, et al.<br>
**Year**: 2022  **Venue**: Human Reproduction Update 29(2); medRxiv preprint<br>
**Original (download)**: [https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2022.03.25.22272822.full.pdf](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2022.03.25.22272822.full.pdf)<br>
**Local PDF**: `[paper] sperm count temporal trends Levine, 2022.pdf` (in this folder)<br>
**Used in**: E15-H76 (male reproductive health - PARTIAL)

## Summary

Global meta-regression of sperm-concentration and sperm-count trends in the 20th and 21st centuries.

## Key findings

- Sperm concentration fell ~51.6% and total count ~62.3% (1973-2018) in the pooled data
- Declines steepest in Western populations; smaller/absent in some non-Western cohorts
- The uniform-worldwide-decline claim is debated and region/cohort-specific

## Relevance to this project

Grounds the PARTIAL verdict on H76: the male reproductive-health decline is real but contested and its fertility magnitude uncertain.
