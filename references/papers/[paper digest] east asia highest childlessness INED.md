## Summary

Short research brief documenting record childlessness in East Asia.

## Key findings

- Childlessness in parts of East Asia is now the highest in the world
- Rising fast among the youngest cohorts (e.g., Korea ~19% for late-1970s cohorts)
- Distinguishes rising East-Asian childlessness from the older high-childlessness pattern in Europe
