# [Paper digest] The Effects of the Dobbs Decision on Fertility

**Authors**: Daniel Dench, Mayra Pineda-Torres, Caitlin Myers<br>
**Year**: 2023  **Venue**: IZA Discussion Paper 16608<br>
**Original (download)**: [https://docs.iza.org/dp16608.pdf](https://docs.iza.org/dp16608.pdf)<br>
**Local PDF**: `[paper] dobbs decision fertility IZA, 2023.pdf` (in this folder)<br>
**Used in**: E15-H60 (contraception/abortion restriction spectrum - lower coercion bound)

## Summary

Difference-in-differences estimate of post-Dobbs abortion-ban effects on US birth rates.

## Key findings

- Ban states had ~2.3% higher fertility in H1 2023 (~32,000 more births)
- Effect concentrated among Hispanic, Black and younger women
- About one in four people seeking an abortion were unable to get one - unwanted births

## Relevance to this project

The moderate-coercion anchor on the H60 spectrum, between a friction lever and Romania's total ban; illustrates that restriction produces unwanted births.
