## Key mechanism

The MLQ is the standard validated instrument for the eudaimonic "presence of meaning" construct that E42 hypothesises loads on fertility. Steger et al. build a 10-item scale in three studies, deliberately separating meaning into two factors - PRESENCE of meaning ("my life has a clear sense of purpose") and SEARCH for meaning ("I am seeking a purpose or mission for my life"). The design innovation is that the items carry NO overlap with mood/distress items, so a meaning score is not just disguised happiness - the key property for E42, which needs meaning to be measurable independently of hedonic affect. Presence-of-meaning is the operational handle for eudaimonic well-being; search-for-meaning is a separate, culturally variable process.

## Main findings

- Two-factor structure (Presence, Search) confirmed by CFA and replicated across three studies; the two subscales are only modestly related, so they are genuinely distinct
- Presence of meaning correlates POSITIVELY with well-being (life satisfaction, optimism, self-esteem, positive affect) and NEGATIVELY with depression; Search correlates weakly or negatively with well-being
- Deliberately decoupled from mood: unlike the older Purpose in Life test (PIL correlated ‒.78 with negative affect, .78 with positive affect - i.e. near-confounded), the MLQ was constructed to strip mood confounding
- Presence and Search are separable enough that a person can score high on both, low on both, or high on one and low on the other - meaning-attained vs meaning-seeking are different states

## Parameters for the model

- **Length / format**: 10 items, two 5-item subscales (Presence, Search); 7-point Likert response (1 = absolutely untrue to 7 = absolutely true)
- **Internal consistency (Cronbach alpha)**: MLQ-Presence 0.81‒0.86; MLQ-Search 0.84‒0.92 (Time 1 to Time 2) - good to excellent reliability
- **Test-retest stability (1 month)**: MLQ-Presence 0.70; MLQ-Search 0.73 - the construct is a stable trait-like disposition, not a transient mood
- **Discriminant validity**: constructed with zero item overlap with distress measures, unlike PIL (‒.78 with negative affect) and LRI - so a meaning score is not a happiness proxy
- **Country-level application**: original validation is US undergraduate + adult samples ONLY; cross-national use comes from later work (Steger et al. 2008, US vs Japan) showing PRESENCE of meaning tends higher in interdependent/collectivist cultures while SEARCH runs higher in individualist ones - directly relevant to placing high-meaning Israel vs the individualist low-TFR regions

## Caveats

- The 2006 paper itself is US-only; treat the cross-country (Japan/collectivist) pattern as a follow-up finding, not established in this PDF - flagged so E42 does not over-claim a validated 8-region meaning ranking exists off the shelf
- Measures INDIVIDUAL meaning; scaling to a population-level "meaning climate" for a region is an inference the project must make, not a number the scale provides
- Search-for-meaning is ambiguous in sign (can reflect growth or deficit) - only PRESENCE should be wired as the eudaimonic-W input to fertility
