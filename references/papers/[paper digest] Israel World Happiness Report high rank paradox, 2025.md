# [Paper digest] Israel's World Happiness Paradox - High Life-Evaluation Despite Conflict

**Author**: Helliwell, Layard, Sachs, De Neve, Aknin, Wang (eds.), World Happiness Report / Gallup World Poll<br>
**Year**: 2025 report (three-year average 2022‒2024)  **Venue**: World Happiness Report, worldhappiness.report<br>
**Original (download)**: [https://www.worldhappiness.report/](https://www.worldhappiness.report/) - report is OA HTML/PDF; the underlying Gallup microdata is proprietary. **Digest-only, no local PDF** (figures cross-checked against the WHR 2025 country table and Times of Israel / Jerusalem Post reporting)<br>
**Used in**: E42 (eudaimonia-vs-hedonia arm - Israel's paradox: high life-evaluation rank co-existing with high TFR and high stress)

## Key mechanism

E42's identification anchor. Israel sits near the TOP of the World Happiness Report's Cantril-ladder ranking despite ongoing conflict, war, and objectively high stress - a paradox the hedonic frame cannot explain. The resolution the round leans on: the WHR "happiness" number is a LIFE-EVALUATION (a ladder judgement heavily loaded on SOCIAL SUPPORT, freedom, and belonging), not a moment-to-moment hedonic mood score. Israel's rank is driven by the eudaimonic/relational subcomponents - strong family and community structure, high social support, a sense of shared purpose and national meaning - the same familism/meaning complex that Okun 2017 and DellaPergola 2020 tie to its anomalously high TFR (2.83). Israel is thus the clean case where HIGH MEANING + HIGH SOCIAL SUPPORT co-occur with HIGH FERTILITY, while its hedonic/affect components (worry, sadness, anger from conflict) are elevated - meaning loads, hedonic mood does not.

## Main findings

- Israel ranks 8th in the world on the Cantril ladder (WHR 2025, 2022‒2024 average) - held its top-10 position through the post-October-2023 war; was 4th in 2023 and 5th in 2024 before the war-year data pulled it to 8th
- The high rank is attributed by the report and commentators to high SOCIAL SUPPORT (having someone to count on), freedom to make life choices, sense of community, and pro-social behaviour (volunteering, giving) - the relational/eudaimonic axis, not consumption or affect
- The WHR score is a life-EVALUATION (ladder), distinct from the report's separate positive-affect (laughter, enjoyment, interest) and negative-affect (worry, sadness, anger) measures - Israel's high evaluation co-exists with conflict-elevated negative affect, the core of the paradox
- Israel's high life-evaluation and high TFR (2.83, highest in the OECD, far above replacement) coincide - the anchor pairing E42 exploits

## Parameters for the model

Cantril-ladder scores (0‒10) and world rank, WHR 2025 (2022‒2024 avg), for the anchor plus the project regions:

- **Israel**: 7.234, rank 8  (TFR ~2.83 - HIGH meaning/support, HIGH fertility)
- **Germany**: 6.753, rank 22
- **Poland**: 6.673, rank 26
- **USA**: 6.724, rank 24
- **France**: 6.593, rank 33
- **Japan**: 6.147, rank 55
- **South Korea**: 6.038, rank 58  (lowest-TFR region, mid-low ladder)
- **Hungary**: 5.915, rank 69

- **Usable structure**: the ladder score is NOT monotonic with TFR across the regions (Germany and USA outrank France yet all are low-TFR; Korea is low on both) - so raw "happiness" does not predict fertility. Israel's position ABOVE all project regions while alone in high TFR isolates the meaning/social-support component as the fertility-relevant axis, not the hedonic-affect component
- **Decomposition to use**: treat the WHR score as life-evaluation loaded on social-support + freedom (eudaimonic-adjacent) and hold it SEPARATE from positive/negative affect (hedonic) - E42 predicts fertility tracks the former, not the latter

## Caveats

- The public WHR gives the composite ladder score and the six explanatory factors' contributions, but the country-by-country subcomponent split (social support vs affect) for Israel is discussed qualitatively in commentary, not published as clean per-country numbers here - the affect-vs-evaluation separation is inferred from the report's methodology, not a downloaded Israel breakdown
- The ladder is self-reported and culturally framed; cross-country ladder comparisons carry known reference-group and translation caveats
- WHR "happiness" conflates evaluation and affect in popular reporting - the paradox only resolves once they are separated; do not read Israel's rank as high hedonic mood
