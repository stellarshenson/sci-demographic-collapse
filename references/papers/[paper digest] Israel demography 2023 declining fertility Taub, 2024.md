## Key mechanism

The anchor source for Israel's TFR decomposed by sector. Weinreb documents that Israel's exceptional fertility is NOT carried by the Haredi tail alone - the ~75% of Jewish Israelis who are secular or traditional sit at or near replacement, far above every other developed secular society. He defines Israel's replacement level as TFR 2.08 (below the usual 2.1 because of low mortality and a low secondary sex ratio). Fertility began falling in 2018 after decades of stability, so the "boom" is now a gentle glide, not a rise.

## Main findings

- National period TFR fell ~0.2 children since 2018; overall ~2.85 in 2023 (CBS), highest in the OECD by a wide margin
- CBS TFR by religion, 2018→2022: Jewish 3.17→3.03, Muslim 3.20→2.91, Christian 2.06→1.68, Druze 2.16→1.85 - only Jews and Muslims remain above replacement
- Weinreb/Hleihel Table 1, period TFR (3-yr centered mean 2020‒2022) of Jewish women by self-identified religiosity: Haredi 6.38, Religious/Dati 3.77, Traditional-religious 2.80, Traditional-not-religious 2.22, Not-religious/secular 1.98
- Change since 2017‒2019: Religious fell most (-0.56), Haredi -0.34, secular -0.13 - the decline is broad, sharpest in the middle
- Secular/not-religious Jewish TFR was 2.09‒2.26 for the whole 2008‒2018 period and only recently dipped just below replacement (1.98) - still the highest secular fertility in the developed world
- Mean age at first birth flat at ~28.6 since 2015 (younger than most of the OECD); IVF-assisted births are a rising share; non-marital births remain rare (~5%)

## Key takeaways

- The transferable puzzle is the SECULAR/traditional near-replacement fertility, not the Haredi outlier - a developed secular society reproducing above the rest of its peer group
- Source `Hleihel (2023)` (CBS demographer Ahmad Hleihel) is the standard reference for TFR-by-religiosity; its weakness is self-reported religiosity and small Haredi/Bedouin samples, cross-checked here against town-level (yishuv) CBS data
- Use TFR 2.85 (2023) as the model "Israel" calibration target; CBS and UN WPP 2024 (2.83) agree, so no CBS-vs-UN conflict here
