# [Paper digest] E27 - Reproductive-concentration structures (polygyny, polyandry, hypergamy, state-sanction, media)

**Arc**: E27-H208..H212 - far-fetched, deliberately controversial fanout on reproductive-concentration structures. Covers BOTH the female side (polygyny: one male, many females) and the male side (polyandry: one female, many males), plus the state-sanctioned vs culturally-normalised vs media-normalisation variants.<br>
**Model fed**: coupled behavioural x Leslie model, TFR = C.(1-rho).Pbar.fec(tau).(1-kBF.dtau), channels {C, rho, Pbar, tau, S, N}, interventions [fS,fC,fPb,fTau,fRV,fN].<br>
**Central crux**: the effective sex ratio / excluded-sex externality on C versus the within-marriage parity (Pbar) and female-childlessness (rho) effect. The multiplicative TFR form means a large C drag can zero out any Pbar gain.

## Why this arc exists

Concentration structures are the sharpest natural test of the sex-ratio pivot the model already uses (Autor et al. 2019; Kearney-Wilson 2018). Polygyny mechanically raises MALE reproductive variance and male childlessness while lowering female childlessness; polyandry does the reverse. The arc asks whether concentrating reproduction on one sex raises aggregate TFR (each partnered woman bears children) or lowers it once the excluded-sex externality on partnership formation (C) and economic security (S) is paid. The honest, unsentimental answer from the evidence is that in a developed low-fertility society the externality on C dominates, and the one structure that reliably moves TFR does so in the WRONG direction (polyandry suppresses).

## H208 - Polygyny (male-concentrated reproduction)

- Within-marriage per-wife fertility is LOWER, not higher: women in polygynous unions are 22-26% less fertile than monogamous women, fertility falls with wife rank (3rd < 2nd < 1st) and rises co-wife count; Kenya and Ivory Coast polygynous wives average ~1 child fewer (Timaeus-Reyner; Bongaarts et al. 1984; PRB 2015). Mechanisms: older husbands, lower coital frequency, spousal separation, subfecundity, longer postpartum abstinence
- High aggregate TFR in polygynous societies (Niger 5.9, Chad 6.0) is a DEVELOPMENT artifact (universal early female marriage, near-zero contraception), not caused by polygyny; declining polygyny has itself CONTRIBUTED to Sahel/West-African fertility DECLINE (Demography 2025 62(5):1717; PRB 2015)
- Institutional counter-force: Tertilt (2005, JPE 113(6):1341) quantitative model finds enforcing monogamy LOWERS fertility 40%, raises savings 70%, raises output/capita 170% - i.e. the polygyny institution (bride-price, early female marriage, women rationed by price) raises fertility in a HIGH-fertility low-development equilibrium. This is the one pro-natal reading, and it is confounded with underdevelopment
- The excluded-male externality is the killer for C: normative/enforced monogamy reduces the pool of unmarried men and thereby cuts crime - rape, murder, assault, robbery, fraud (Henrich, Boyd, Richerson 2012, Phil Trans R Soc B 367:657); Edlund, Li, Yi, Zhang (2013, REStat 95(5):1520) find a 0.01 rise in the sex ratio raises violent+property crime ~3%, elasticity of crime to the 16-25 male sex ratio ~3.4, ~1/7 of China's crime rise; Hudson-den Boer (2004, Bare Branches) tie "surplus low-status young males" to societal instability
- Verdict-lean REFUTED (as a collapse-reversal lever in a developed low-fertility society): per-wife Pbar is DOWN and the excluded-male drag on C and S is severe - a double loss under the multiplicative form. The high-TFR-in-Sahel correlation is development-confounded and does not transplant. Sign is context-dependent (Tertilt's +40% holds only in the low-development, female-marriage-constrained regime)

## H209 - Polyandry (female-side concentration)

- Individual female fertility is UNCHANGED by the marital form (polyandrous women bear at monogamous rates), but AGGREGATE fertility is significantly DEPRESSED because multiple brothers share one wife, cutting the share of reproductive women per generation (Goldstein 1976, Human Ecology 4:223; Levine-Ross; Childs 2003)
- Goldstein: fraternal polyandry "functions, unperceived and unintended, as an important mechanism for reducing population growth"; aggregate birthrates track strict-vs-lax adherence to polyandry - a land/resource-ceiling adaptation preventing subdivision of family land
- The female reproductive bottleneck produces surplus UNMARRIED WOMEN (the "other side of polyandry" - non-marriage, nunhood; Levine), i.e. female childlessness UP - the mechanical mirror of polygyny's excluded men
- Verdict-lean SUPPORTED (that polyandry SUPPRESSES TFR): robust, low-leakage, mechanically driven by rho_female up with Pbar unchanged. For demographic collapse this is the WRONG direction - polyandry deepens the decline; it is a population brake, not a lever

## H210 - Extreme hypergamy / mating-market skew (no formal polygyny)

- Online dating reveals a pronounced desirability hierarchy: both sexes pursue partners ~25% more desirable than themselves, and response probability falls sharply with the desirability gap (Bruch-Newman 2018, Science Advances 4:eaap9815) - de-facto top-male concentration without any institution
- The bottom of the male distribution and the top of the female distribution fail to match (compounded by the end of educational hypergamy - women now match down or not at all; Esteve et al. 2016, Pop Dev Rev 42:615), producing a soft version of the excluded-sex externality: matching friction, later partnering, involuntary singlehood
- No credible causal estimate links dating-app skew to TFR; the fertility effect is inferred, not measured (thin parameter - bound, do not invent)
- Verdict-lean PARTIAL: a plausible C drag plus tempo delay, but unquantified; a diffuse, self-correcting (people settle) version of the excluded-male externality without institutional lock-in

## H211 - State-sanctioned vs culturally-normalised vs outlawed

- State sanction/subsidy acts through N (legitimacy, norm endorsement) and S (subsidy) on top of the base structure; enforced monogamy is itself the cultural-evolutionary package that suppresses the unmarried-men externality (Henrich et al. 2012)
- Legal-polygyny states (Sahel, parts of the Gulf) show high TFR, but once development is controlled the legal switch is not the cause - the correlation is confounded by underdevelopment and universal early female marriage (Tertilt 2005; Demography 2025). Legal sanction changes the MAGNITUDE of both the within-marriage fertility channel and the excluded-male externality, not the SIGN
- Verdict-lean PARTIAL/REFUTED: sanction entrenches the externality institutionally; it cannot manufacture the missing quantum in a developed low-fertility society. State outlaw (enforced monogamy) is the historically fertility-and-stability-favouring institution in the developed regime

## H212 - Media / normalisation forcing on N

- Media-normalisation is a pure forcing on the bistable norm state N with no material lever (no housing, income, or partner-supply change). Greater mass-media exposure is empirically associated with LOWER fertility, not higher (HIP social-norms brief; fertility-transition literature), and modern media diffuses SMALL-family ideals
- Fertility is genuinely contagious in social networks (Balbo-Barban 2014, ASR 79:412; sibling/peer spillovers), so N-forcing is real - but the direction media pushes is toward low fertility, and the excluded-male media ecosystem (incel discourse) is itself a media-amplified C-destabiliser
- Verdict-lean REFUTED: normalising the concentration structure via media does not move TFR net of the sex-ratio externality; N-forcing without a material channel is cheap talk that reverts under the hysteresis of N, and the incel externality drags C

## Inter-channel interaction (the core C-vs-Pbar tension of the whole arc)

The multiplicative form TFR = C.(1-rho).Pbar.fec(tau) is what decides every hypothesis. Concentration trades a female-side rho/Pbar effect against a C effect through the excluded sex:

- Polygyny: female rho DOWN (more women partnered) but per-wife Pbar DOWN 22-26% (co-wife effect) AND male-exclusion drags C and S. Double loss - the Pbar term does not even help, and C falls. Net: not a lever
- Polyandry: Pbar unchanged, female rho UP (surplus unmarried women), C down. Clean suppression - the mechanism that reliably moves TFR moves it downward
- Because C sits as a leading multiplicative factor and is bistable below ~0.66, a moderate excluded-sex externality on C can zero out a within-marriage Pbar gain. Across the arc the excluded-sex externality on C dominates the within-marriage fertility gain - which is exactly why most verdicts are REFUTED or PARTIAL, and the only SUPPORTED result is a suppressor. The honest reading: reproductive concentration is a population brake or a wash in the developed low-fertility regime, not a route out of the coupling trap

## Verdict mix

H208 REFUTED (context-dependent sign), H209 SUPPORTED as a suppressor (wrong direction for collapse), H210 PARTIAL, H211 PARTIAL/REFUTED, H212 REFUTED. Net: the arc confirms the model's sex-ratio pivot - the excluded-sex externality on C is the binding term, and no concentration structure lifts TFR in a developed low-fertility society net of it.
