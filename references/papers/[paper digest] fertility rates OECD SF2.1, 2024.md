# [Paper digest] SF2.1 Fertility Rates

**Authors**: OECD Family Database<br>
**Year**: 2024  **Venue**: OECD Social Policy Division<br>
**Original (download)**: [https://webfs.oecd.org/els-com/Family_Database/SF_2_1_Fertility_rates.pdf](https://webfs.oecd.org/els-com/Family_Database/SF_2_1_Fertility_rates.pdf)<br>
**Local PDF**: `[paper] fertility rates OECD SF2.1, 2024.pdf` (in this folder)<br>
**Used in**: E15-H58 (mean-age-at-birth trend), general context

## Summary

Cross-country total fertility rate and mean-age-at-childbearing series.

## Key findings

- TFR has fallen well below replacement across the OECD
- Mean age at first birth has risen ~0.08 years per calendar year since 1970, now ~28-29

## Relevance to this project

Context for the postponement trend and the H58 caveat that the secular trend runs toward later, not earlier, union and birth.
