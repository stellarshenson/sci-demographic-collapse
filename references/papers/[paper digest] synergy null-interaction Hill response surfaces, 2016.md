# Theory of synergistic effects: Hill-type response surfaces as 'null-interaction' models for mixtures

**2016** (Theor. Biol. Med. Model.). Europe PMC PMC5541435. Open access.

Download: https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC5541435?pdf=render

## What it establishes
- A response surface decomposes into a NULL-INTERACTION baseline plus a DEPARTURE that IS the
  interaction (synergy > 0 / antagonism < 0). Defines synergy as deviation from a multiplicative /
  Loewe-additive null model - the drug-combination analogue of our fertility-channel law.
- The natural null model for effects bounded in (0,1) that COMPOSE is MULTIPLICATIVE (independent
  survival/inhibition fractions multiply); on the log scale that null is ADDITIVE. Synergy is any
  measured departure from log-additivity - not the curvature of the response transform itself.
- Grounds the central correction in our doc: apparent super-additivity on the RAW effect scale is an
  artifact of the multiplicative (concave-in-effect) null, NOT interaction. Interaction must be read on
  the log/response scale where the null is additive.

## Grounds
The choice of log-TFR as the scale on which the honest combination law is additive, and the definition
of interaction as the departure from the multiplicative (Bongaarts) null.
