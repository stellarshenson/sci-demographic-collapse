## Summary

Korea's single-exam, private-tutoring education tournament and its link to the birth rate.

## Key findings

- The Suneung exam can determine a life; ~85,000 hagwons enroll 78% of students
- Private-education spending reached 27.1 trillion won (~21 billion USD) in 2023; 'edupoverty' hits 4 in 10 families
- High education cost is widely linked to the 0.72 birth rate; Korea has begun cutting exam 'killer questions'

## Relevance to this project

Grounds H88/H89: capping the hagwon arms race and de-determinizing the single exam lowers the ruinous cost of a 'competitive' child. Reporting - link to source.
