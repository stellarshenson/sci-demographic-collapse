# [Paper digest] Does Parental Leave Affect Fertility and Return-to-Work?

**Authors**: Rafael Lalive, Josef Zweimuller<br>
**Year**: 2005  **Venue**: IZA Discussion Paper 1613<br>
**Original (download)**: [https://docs.iza.org/dp1613.pdf](https://docs.iza.org/dp1613.pdf)<br>
**Local PDF**: `[paper] parental leave fertility return to work IZA, 2005.pdf` (in this folder)<br>
**Used in**: E15-H57 (parental-leave lever)

## Summary

Regression-discontinuity evaluation of an Austrian parental-leave extension on subsequent fertility and employment.

## Key findings

- Longer paid leave raises higher-order fertility
- It also affects mothers' return-to-work timing
- Effects operate through the reduced cost/opportunity-cost of an additional child

## Relevance to this project

Grounds the parental-leave lever in the strength catalogue (H57) as a durable, intensive-margin quantum effect.
