## Summary

Natural experiment: free/low-cost long-acting reversible contraception (IUDs, implants) provided through Title X clinics.

## Key findings

- Teen birth and abortion rates fell ~40% and ~35% (2009-2013)
- Within 7 miles of a clinic, births to 15-19-year-olds fell ~20%
- Up to two-thirds of the decline attributed to the initiative

## Relevance to this project

Runs the H60 friction mechanism in reverse: expanding long-acting methods sharply cut births, so removing them (leaving only per-act condoms) would raise them. PDF render-blocked - link to PMC.
