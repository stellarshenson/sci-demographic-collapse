**Authors**: OECD Family Database<br>
**Year**: 2024  **Venue**: OECD Social Policy Division<br>
**Original (download)**: [https://webfs.oecd.org/els-com/Family_Database/SF_2_5_Childlessness.pdf](https://webfs.oecd.org/els-com/Family_Database/SF_2_5_Childlessness.pdf)<br>

**Used in**: E15-H52 (extensive-margin anchors; the per-region childlessness RHO values)

## Summary

Cross-country statistics on completed cohort childlessness among women.

## Key findings

- Cohort childlessness ~28% for the 1970s cohort in Germany, Italy and Japan
- ~10% in France and below ~13% in the US - two to three times lower
- Childlessness roughly doubled across many countries since the 1950s cohorts

## Relevance to this project

Supplies the literature childlessness anchors (RHO) that pin the extensive margin per region in the TFR = (1-rho)*Pbar decomposition (H52).
